01 About

I'm a Remote Sensing and GIS specialist who builds climate, agriculture and land-use analytics from multi-temporal, multi-sensor satellite and drone imagery. My work covers vegetation analysis (NDVI, SAVI, MSAVI), Ecosystem Services Prioritization, change detection, Flood Mapping, and time-series modelling.

Recently I have built a carbon dMRV system from the ground up, combining spatial modelling, reproducible pipelines, QA/QC rigour and dashboards, and I present results to senior decision makers while working closely with Product and Engineering teams.
