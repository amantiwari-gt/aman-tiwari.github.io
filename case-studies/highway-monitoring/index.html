TOOLS & METHODS

Drone data · GIS · Esri Web AppBuilder

01

The context

The NHAI Drone Analytics Monitoring System supported multi-temporal mapping and change detection across an 11,000+ km national highway monitoring program. GIS workflows connected drone-derived observations with outputs used in infrastructure monitoring and decision-making.

02

My contribution

I led GIS workflows within DAMS. My contribution covered multi-temporal mapping and change detection, geospatial data-processing workflows, quality assurance, and delivery. The 11,000+ km figure describes the monitoring program supported, rather than a claim of sole responsibility for the full system.

03

Dashboard delivery

I developed and deployed interactive Esri Web AppBuilder dashboards supporting internal leadership, NHAI, and partner stakeholders. Their purpose was to make geospatial monitoring outputs accessible in the decision-making workflow.

04

Coordinating the GIS work

I directly managed 35 GIS interns, overseeing task allocation, data-processing workflows, quality assurance, and delivery. This was a coordination and technical quality responsibility within the GIS delivery effort.

05

Outcome and scope

The delivered GIS workflows and dashboards supported infrastructure monitoring and decision-making. This case covers geospatial delivery within the wider DAMS program, including the people, processing, and quality responsibilities involved.

OWNERSHIP & PUBLIC MATERIAL
