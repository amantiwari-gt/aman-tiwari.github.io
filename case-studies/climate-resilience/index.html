01

Two related contributions

At CEEW, my GIS work contributed to two distinct efforts: heat-stress analysis supporting Thane’s Heat Action Plan, and the design of the Climate Resilience Atlas for India. They are described separately to preserve the scope of each contribution.

02

Thane Heat Action Plan

I used Google Earth Engine and Python for spatial and temporal heat-stress analysis contributing to the Heat Action Plan for Thane. The contribution was analytical; the wider plan and its policy process were collaborative work.

03

Climate Resilience Atlas for India

I helped design the atlas, bringing together 15+ geospatial layers for district-level climate-risk analysis and decision support. The layer count describes the atlas contribution and does not imply sole ownership of its design or the wider platform.

04

Technical focus

The work involved spatial and temporal analysis, raster/vector data, and the integration of multiple geospatial layers. These are different analytical tasks: exploring heat stress over space and time, and bringing spatial layers together in a decision-support context.

05

Outcome and limits

The work contributed geospatial analysis to climate-risk planning and atlas design. This portfolio does not claim sole authorship of either deliverable or attribute a quantified policy outcome to my individual contribution.
